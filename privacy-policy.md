# Privacy Policy for The Elements

*Last updated: November 2026*

**The Elements** is a game for children aged 6 to 12, made by an independent developer. We take children's privacy
seriously, so the game is built to collect nothing.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** The game does not ask for, collect, store on a server, or share any personal information:
- no names, email addresses, photos, contacts or location;
- no accounts, logins, chat or messages;
- no advertising and no advertising IDs;
- no analytics, tracking or crash-reporting services.

Your game progress (levels, coins, Elements, settings) is saved **only on your device**. Uninstalling the game
deletes it.

## Purchases

The game is free to download. A single optional purchase, the **Full Game**, unlocks more worlds. Purchases are
handled entirely by **Google Play**; we never see or receive payment details. Google Play tells the game only
whether the Full Game has been bought, so it can be unlocked (and restored on a new device). Before any purchase,
the game asks a grown-up to answer a question (a parent gate). Google's own privacy policy applies to Google Play:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Internet

The game plays fully offline. It only connects to Google Play to check or make the Full Game purchase.

## Children

The game is designed for children and follows Google Play's Families policy. Because no personal information is
collected, none can be shared or sold.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
